## How Much Sleep Adults Need

Most adults need seven or more hours a night [^1]. A steady wake time matters as much as the total [^1].

## Light in the Evening

Bright evening light pushes melatonin later [^2], which is why screens before bed make it harder to fall asleep [^2].
